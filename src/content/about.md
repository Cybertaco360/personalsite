# About This Site

Hiiii! This is my own little site is my public notebook & Library, a place where I document projects, research, the ideas I'm exploring and everything in between. Some notes are polished beautifully, others are... works in progress. Together, they document what I'm learning, building, and curious about over the years. And I quote: 

> _"This is a garden, not a museum."_

## Colophon, Purpose, and Background

This site was made in Astro.js using Twilight by Spr-Aachen as the main theme, any custom components that was not in the original template is written in Vue.js by me while the original content is written in Svelte. I've honestly wanted to make a notes blog ever since I was 14; there was always something magical about stumbling across these hidden gems on the internet. They weren't polished like research papers or textbooks, but they were written with a kind of passion that made them feel genuine. After I had received my acceptance and high school diploma, I finally began to work on this site. To this day there are some notes that still absolutely amaze me, such as ["You can replace i with −i in the Schrödinger equation?" by UlyssesZhan](https://ulysseszh.github.io/physics/2023/10/18/se-conjugate.html), and ["Typescript 小技巧" by hyrious](https://hyrious.me/p/typescript-tips) that were also inspirations to me creating my own note blog. I don't know if my notes will ever have the same impact on someone else, but I'd be happy if even one post ends up being someone else's "hidden gem". (And yes, I write with an oxford comma, deal with it XD. )
